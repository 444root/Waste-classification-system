Classify a waste item — experimental 6-category preview

This is a real, trained model that tries to tell apart cardboard, glass, metal, paper, plastic and trash — not just paper vs. not paper. Its held-out test accuracy is 78.6%, which does not clear this project's own 85% threshold for a production model, so results here are not saved to your history and don't count against your quota. Treat it as a preview of work in progress, not a finished feature. Some categories are much more reliable than others (cardboard and paper are the strongest; trash is the weakest, mainly because it's the smallest and most visually varied category in the training data).
